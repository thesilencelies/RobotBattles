## Game rules
At no point is an agent permitted to make a decision that covers the space of game design itself.
Where a rule is unclear or ambiguous, ask, don't assume.
Where rules updates are given, apply them exactly as said.

Agents are permitted to enforce consistency when applying rules updates, but again they must ask before making the changes.

## Card designs
Agents can present analysis and findings about the state of different cards, but may not make any changes
to the card designs outside of explicit instruction to do so.
