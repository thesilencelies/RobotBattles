[7] GAME BALANCE & DESIGN TUNING SUMMARY
================================================================================
A. First-Contact Lethality:
   - Bloodsport Bar (Spin 3-4 = 18-24 Dmg) and Horizontal Spinner (Spin 3-4 = 12-16 Dmg)
     instantly obliterate almost every internal component (durability 5-7).
   - At high spin-ups, one-hit kills dominate over progressive damage.

B. Defensive Options (Armor & Wedges):
   - UHMW Armor (Dur 12, Abs 4) and Titanium Wedges (Dur 12, Abs 4) withstand hits up to 8-11 Dmg.
   - Against 16-24 Dmg, armor is destroyed on the first hit, and excess damage penetrates directly to batteries/motors.
   - TPU Plates (Abs 5) are extremely effective against vertical spinners and lower-tier weapons.

C. Feedback & Self-Destruction Risk:
   - Horizontal Spinner destroys ITSELF at Spin 4 (16 Dmg -> 8 Recoil > 7 Durability).
   - Bloodsport Bar destroys ITSELF at Spin 4 (24 Dmg -> 12 Recoil > 9 Durability).
   - Drive pushing feedback (0.25 - 1.5 Dmg) is safely absorbed by drive wheels with 0 damage.
   - Throw Shock equal to throw strength is lethal: hitting an unarmored battery or motor (Dur 5-7)
     bypasses all perimeter armor and causes catastrophic internal failure.

[7] GAME BALANCE & DESIGN TUNING SUMMARY
================================================================================
A. First-Contact Lethality:
   - Bloodsport Bar (Spin 3-4 = 18-24 Dmg) and Horizontal Spinner (Spin 3-4 = 12-16 Dmg)
     instantly obliterate almost every internal component (durability 5-7).
   - At high spin-ups, one-hit kills dominate over progressive damage.

B. Defensive Options (Armor & Wedges):
   - UHMW Armor (Dur 12, Abs 4) and Titanium Wedges (Dur 12, Abs 4) withstand hits up to 8-11 Dmg.
   - Against 16-24 Dmg, armor is destroyed on the first hit, and excess damage penetrates directly to batteries/motors.
   - TPU Plates (Abs 5) are extremely effective against vertical spinners and lower-tier weapons.

C. Feedback & Self-Destruction Risk:
   - Horizontal Spinner destroys ITSELF at Spin 4 (16 Dmg -> 8 Recoil > 7 Durability).
   - Bloodsport Bar destroys ITSELF at Spin 4 (24 Dmg -> 12 Recoil > 9 Durability).
   - Drive pushing feedback (0.25 - 1.5 Dmg) is safely absorbed by drive wheels with 0 damage.
   - Throw Shock equal to throw strength is lethal: hitting an unarmored battery or motor (Dur 5-7)
     bypasses all perimeter armor and causes catastrophic internal failure.
